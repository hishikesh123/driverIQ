## How the engine refuses

A window needs at least 10 recorded occurrences to be ranked. Below that it is reported but not ranked; with no history at all the verdict is `not_enough_evidence` and **no dollar figure is produced**. Declining to answer is a feature here — without it the engine would rank a window seen twice against one seen fifty times.

Worth being straight about which half of that gate actually fires on this dataset: every window the driver has worked has at least 14 occurrences, so the thin-evidence branch is never reached. The refusals above all come from windows with *no* history at all — morning and overnight. The threshold is there for a future export, or another driver, where it would bite.

## Caveats carried with every answer

- Historical estimates from this driver's own records — they carry no information about current demand, driver supply, incentives or surge.
- Figures describe earnings in a window on days the driver chose to work it, so they bundle productivity with how long they typically stay.
- The driver selects when to work, so genuinely bad windows are under-represented: treat a low figure as 'rarely worth it', not as a forecast.
